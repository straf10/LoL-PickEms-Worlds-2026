# Worlds 2024 / 2025 base rates for the Crystal Ball (T1.4b)

Source: `data/pickems.duckdb` (read-only), league `WLDs` only; `WLDQ` regional qualifiers excluded. Generated by `python -m crystal.base_rates`. Tables: `outputs/base_rates/*.csv`.

- **2024**: 106 games, 50 series, patch 14.18, Play-In/Swiss break at 2024-10-03; stage games: Play-In 24, Swiss 54, QF 16, SF 7, F 5.
- **2025**: 84 games, 41 series, patch 15.20, Play-In/Swiss break at 2025-10-22; stage games: Play-In 35, Swiss 21, QF 16, SF 7, F 5.

## Stage and placement method

- Series = consecutive `game_in_series` for one team pair on one UTC day. Bo1 = single-game series.
- Knockouts = last 7 series in time order (QF x4, SF x2, Final). The `playoffs` flag is False for every Worlds game, so it is not used.
- Play-In vs Swiss = first day after the first gap of 2+ days in the pre-knockout schedule.
- Placement = furthest stage reached: Champion, Finalist, SF (3rd-4th), QF (5th-8th), Swiss exit, Play-In exit.
- Presence (Q1) = share of games where the champion was picked or banned (each game counts once). The raw (picks+bans)/games ratio is in the CSV as `appearances_per_game`.
- 2025 caveat: the Oct 14-19 block (35 games, 17 teams) holds 3-win qualifiers who go straight to the knockouts (KT Rolster, Anyone's Legend), so its label 'Play-In' means block 1, not the 2024-style Play-In. The Oct 22-25 block (21 games, 12 teams) is labelled 'Swiss'. The 'Play-In exit' and 'Swiss exit' labels are therefore only block labels for 2025. Knockout teams and the final placements are not affected.
