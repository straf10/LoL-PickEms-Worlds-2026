## 6. Data gaps and caveats

- Unmatched change names (not in DB champion list): none.
- Patch 16.16 (Riot 26.16) notes were not fetched, so nothing before 26.17 is encoded.
- Patch labels in the DB are noisy: 16.17 and 16.18 extend past their Riot release dates, and 16.19 is missing. The 16.18 label covers Riot 26.18 and 26.19 games.
- Third-party reports of Orianna and Sylas/Varus changes were not in the official notes and are not encoded.
- Riot 26.16 notes (16.16) were not fetched, so SR changes before 26.17 are not encoded. 16.16 games were played before 26.17, so only the 26.17+ changes apply to them.
- The DB patch labels are noisy: 16.17 and 16.18 run past their Riot release dates (16.18 covers late 26.18 and most of 26.19), and 16.19 is missing.
- Worlds 2025 has 84 games in the DB, which may be incomplete; this affects the Worlds game count W and the Q10/Q11 Worlds references.
- Meta score weights are heuristics, not fitted. The early read is any-league (minor leagues included).
- Teemo and the Q9 base rate use all-league 2026 games; the pick-count denominator is approximate.
- Third-party-only claims (Orianna, Sylas/Varus) are not encoded.
